/unrobot

Makes a text read as if a person wrote it

Machine-written text gives itself away: grand closing lines, inflated significance, brochure tone, em dashes. This skill finds those marks in a text, a file, a folder or a whole site and rewrites the passages so they read as if a person who knows the subject wrote them.

It builds on the Wikipedia article "Signs of AI writing" and leaves wording you fixed on purpose alone. Its scanner needs Python 3 and reads English only.

Start it with /unrobot, or ask to make a text "sound less like AI". Unzip it into your skills folder.

Download ZIP Read SKILL.md

When the agent uses it

Turn text into prose that carries no marks of machine writing, based on the Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing" catalogue plus further prose tics (dramatic one-line closers, deep-sounding sayings, repeated sentence starts, "not X, but Y", inflated significance, brochure tone, vague attribution, em dashes, bold-label bullets, chatbot leftovers). Use when text should be de-AI'd, humanised, made "less robotic", or checked for AI tells before publishing: blog posts, web copy, emails, docs, game text, whole sites. Also use when the user asks to make text "sound less like AI" or to "remove the AI tells", or says a draft sounds like AI. Not for proofreading, grammar fixes, translation or tone changes unless AI tells are the stated concern.

Install

Download the skill, drag it into your AI's chat and write "Install this skill."
